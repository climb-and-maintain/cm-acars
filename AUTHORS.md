# Authors and contributors

## Original author and steward

**Climb and Maintain, SPC** is the original author and initial project steward of Climb and Maintain ACARS.

## Contributors

Git history and accepted pull requests are the authoritative contributor record. Contributors retain attribution for their work under the repository license and any applicable third-party license.

Imported or adapted aircraft profiles preserve their upstream author metadata in the profile and in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Do not replace upstream authorship with project branding.

Forks may use their own product branding while retaining required historical and legal attribution. See [Forking and branding](docs/FORKING-AND-BRANDING.md).
