# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, licensing, attribution, and accessibility needs.
- Accept maintainers' good-faith moderation decisions.
- Report safety, security, or conduct concerns privately when public discussion could cause harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Pressuring users to upload credentials, proprietary simulator files, aircraft files, or private diagnostics.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose conduct is inappropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's published private contact route. If the concern involves a maintainer, identify that conflict so it can be handled by another steward where possible. Reports will be reviewed promptly, kept as confidential as practical, and handled without retaliation.

Maintainers should consider context, impact, intent, prior conduct, and the safety of affected people when choosing a response.
